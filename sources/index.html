Sources

Every numbered citation in the essay points here. Links go to the original reporting, the Code of Virginia, the 2023 bill record, or the Hingham documents used only as illustration. If a claim cannot be sourced, it is not in the essay.

Two details in contemporaneous coverage conflict—how many days passed before a warrant issued—and are therefore omitted rather than split-the-difference. The June 5 date, the charge, the bench conviction, the suspended sentence, and the seven-year registry placement are reported in multiple independent outlets.

Statutes & regulations

  1. 10

    Code of Virginia

    § 18.2-371. Causing or encouraging acts rendering children delinquent, abused, etc.

    The Class 1 misdemeanor under which Parkinson was convicted. It incorporates the juvenile-code definition of “abused or neglected” in § 16.1-228.

    Open source
  2. 11

    Code of Virginia

    § 63.2-100. Definitions — “abused or neglected child,” independent activities

    Social-services definition. The independent-activity carve-out is the statutory heart of this essay.

    Open source
  3. 12

    Code of Virginia

    § 16.1-228. Definitions (juvenile and domestic relations)

    The juvenile-code definition of “abused or neglected child,” which SB 1367 also amended. § 18.2-371 points here, not merely to the social-services title.

    Open source
  4. 13

    Virginia Administrative Code

    22VAC40-705-30. Types of abuse and neglect

    DSS regulation repeating the independent-activity carve-out in the physical-neglect section.

    Open source
